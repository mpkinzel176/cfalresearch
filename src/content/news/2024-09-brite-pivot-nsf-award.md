headline: "New NSF award funds low-gravity functional sensor research"
summary: "CFAL received a $600,000 NSF award (BRITE PIVOT) to develop new functional sensors based on light-matter interactions for use in low-gravity space environments."
category: "grant"
---

CFAL received a $600,000 award from the National Science Foundation for the project *BRITE PIVOT: Leveraging Light-Matter Interactions to Create New Functional Sensors in Low-Gravity Space Environments*, running September 2024 through August 2027.
